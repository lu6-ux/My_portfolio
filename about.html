About Me

The short version, and the not-so-short version.

I'm Lakshana Gnanasekaram, an undergraduate reading for a Bachelor of Technology (Hons) in ICT, specializing in Artificial Intelligence, at the University of Colombo.

I've spent the last year working across backend development and applied AI — building APIs, wiring up machine learning models, and shipping small AI-powered tools that solve real problems: PDF question-answering, resume matching, and recommendation systems among them.

I care about the full pipeline of a project — from cleaning and exploring data, to training a model, to putting it behind a backend that's actually reliable in production.

Outside of code, I enjoy design work in Figma and Illustrator, which shows up in how I think about the UI layer of what I build.
